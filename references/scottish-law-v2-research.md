# Scottish-law V2 Will research notes

The Scottish V2 option must remain separate from the existing England and Wales template. The implementation therefore uses a Scottish-law title, testamentary-writing revocation language, a Scottish legal-rights and special-destinations clause, Scottish intestacy fallback wording, and Scottish execution guidance.

Key findings:

- The Scottish Government identifies the Succession (Scotland) Act 1964 as the principal inheritance legislation and notes the distinct Scottish succession framework. [1]
- The Scottish Parliament’s 2025 briefing explains that legal rights can apply to a spouse/civil partner and children against the moveable estate, even where a Will exists. [2]
- HMRC’s current Scottish Wills guidance states that Wills executed on or after 1 August 1995 are governed by the Requirements of Writing (Scotland) Act 1995; a subscribed Will must be signed on the last page (and each page of a multi-page Will), while a Will attested by one witness is self-evidencing. [3]

The Scottish-law document and associated signing material must be reviewed by a qualified Scots-law solicitor before execution.
